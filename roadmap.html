A working game.
Room to grow.

The career, transfers, training, youth, competitions and 2D/3D matches already work. The next steps build on that foundation.

What you can play today

A management career across 22 leagues and 440 clubs, with domestic and continental football, national teams, financial decisions, scouting, transfers and player development. Matches can be watched in 2D/3D or played with direct control.

First priority: the player experience

Improve navigation, readable layouts, tables and charts, clear decisions, keyboard access and first-career guidance. We want newcomers to feel comfortable managing their club, and regular players to spend less time finding information.

Create your own football world

A future in-game editor should make custom worlds approachable, with previews, validation and shareable data packs.

  • Create and edit players, clubs, staff, venues, countries and national associations.
  • Build club leagues and cups, continental competitions, national-team qualifiers and international tournaments.
  • Configure formats, qualification routes, calendars, eligibility, tie-breakers and promotion or relegation.
  • Invite other clubs to friendlies, arrange matches for a charity or community cause, and organise summer and preseason friendly tournaments.
  • Manage replies, venues, dates and scheduling conflicts before confirming fixtures.

A community beyond the code

This is a separate, nontechnical direction: welcome players and creators, share careers and custom worlds, organise playtests and community challenges, and recognise help with guides, translations, data and feedback. Start with a manageable community space and clear participation guidelines.

Keep improving the football

  • Match balance: conversion, possession differences and offside realism.
  • A practice match and fuller direct control over set pieces.
  • Season calendars for Southern-hemisphere and American competitions.
  • More national-team matchday control and player/contract depth.
  • Stronger reproducibility across platforms, and release validation.
  • More match audio and smoother finishing touches.

Everything in these future directions is planned, not an available feature or a promised release date. The detailed roadmap separates the editor stages and community work. For a current task, start with the issues and discuss the change with the maintainer.
