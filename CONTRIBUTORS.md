## Add your name

Include a change to this file with your contribution. Add one bullet with the
name or handle you want displayed, optionally linked to a public profile.
Keep the list alphabetical and add yourself once. You can use a nickname;
credit here is optional. Code, documentation, translations, game data,
playtesting and bug investigation all count.

You may also add your name as a fictional player in your favorite club under
`assets/user_made_data/players/`. Follow the
[player cameo walkthrough](docs/contributing/player-cameo.md) for a complete
JSON example. You can choose either kind of credit, both, or neither.

See the [contributor guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) for your first pull request.
