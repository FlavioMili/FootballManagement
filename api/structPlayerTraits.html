Detailed Description

Hidden personality and physical traits on a 1-100 scale.

Professionalism scales training effort and slows decline, ambition drives expectations about playing time and wages, temperament damps morale swings (high = composed), loyalty softens reactions to transfer interest and injury proneness scales the injury hazard (50 = average).
