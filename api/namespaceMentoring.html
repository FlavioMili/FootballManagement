Detailed Description

Bounded, slow personality transfer from mentor to youngster.

Personality changes little in young adulthood: meta-analyses of longitudinal studies find mean-level shifts of a fraction of a standard deviation per decade, larger in the late teens and early twenties, with social roles (a first job, a mentor) among the drivers (Roberts, Walton & Viechtbauer 2006). Each week a mentee's professionalism and temperament move a small share of the gap towards the mentor's, scaled by the youngster's receptiveness (age) and the mentor's standing (age and experience), at most 0.1 points a week (about five a season) and 10 points in total. A professional mentor also adds up to 4% to the youngster's development; an unhappy mentor spreads his mood instead.
