Detailed Description

Final outcome of one competition in one season.

League rows carry champion, runner-up, the clubs promoted out of the league and relegated out of it, and the league top scorer. Cup rows (competition_type == CUP, competition_id == country root league) carry the cup winner as champion and the losing finalist as runner-up.
