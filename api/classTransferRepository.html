Detailed Description

Persists the transfer market: history (append-only), payments and clauses, loans, pre-contracts, player market flags, incoming offers with the rounds of their talks, and the managed club's open talks.

Everything but the history is small and rewritten on every save; all writes run inside the caller's transaction.
