Detailed Description

National-team calendar, call-ups, matches and records.

Matches are simulated with the match engine through the same MatchScheduler batch as club matches. Called-up players are unavailable to their clubs from the Monday of a window to its last day (finals: until their nation is out), come back with the match fatigue and injuries of the engine plus travel fatigue, and raise the injury risk for a few days after long trips. Clubs are compensated per player and day for finals.
