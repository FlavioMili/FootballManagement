Detailed Description

A measurable commitment with a deadline.

PlayingTime: player_minutes / team_minutes >= target over the window (minutes the player was injured do not count against it). NewContract: contract years above baseline. Signing: a signing whose overall is at least target (the club's 11th best overall when promised).
