Detailed Description

Revenue, cost and attendance model of a club.

Revenue is split into TV, continental prizes, gate and commercial income with the league's ECFIL-style mix. Richer, more reputable clubs earn more commercial and gate income while TV money is shared mostly equally.

Attendance of a home match: capacity * interest * price factor, at most the capacity. interest = max(core, demand * exp(a * success)) for the opponent and competition; price factor = exp(-e * ln(r) - k * max(0, r - 1)^2) with r = price / ref, so gate income peaks a little above the reference price and an overpriced ticket empties the ground.

Parameters
successLeague position and form signal in [-1, 1].

double ClubEconomy::expectedIncome ( const LeagueEconomy &  economy,
std::uint8_t  reputation

Income the club can budget on per season: equal TV share, merit money at its expected finish, gate and commercial income, plus half of the continental money of its expected place (boards do not spend prize money they may miss). Every cost line and budget is a share of it, so a club that finishes where expected roughly breaks even; beating expectations or reaching continental places makes a profit.
