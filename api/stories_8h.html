enum class StoryKind : std::uint8_t
strong

Story chains that grow out of what actually happens in the save.

Each story is triggered by a real event of the managed club (a debut, a string of high ratings, a long injury ending, repeated bids for a key player...) and produces at most one inbox message per instance. Stories that call for a decision carry a StoryChoice: the inbox offers to talk to the player with the conversation options that answer it. A weekly cap keeps busy periods from flooding the inbox.

The kinds after Rivalry are decision moments: short two-option dilemmas (see Dilemma) answered from the inbox. Values are persisted: append only.
