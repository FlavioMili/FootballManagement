Detailed Description

How an AI club negotiates to buy one of the managed club's players, and how the player feels about the move.

The buyer has a hidden ceiling on what the deal may cost it, measured by buyerCost(): cash now counts in full, instalments are discounted, add-ons cost their odds of being paid and a sell-on clause the expected share of a future sale. It answers the club's counters with its own structures (a higher fee spread over more years, add-ons instead of cash, a smaller sell-on), loses patience after a few rounds, walks away from demands far beyond its ceiling, pays more against rival bidders and near the deadline. Pure rules: every draw is passed in, so the answers are deterministic.
