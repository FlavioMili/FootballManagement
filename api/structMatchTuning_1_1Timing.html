◆ BACKGROUND_STOPPAGE_TICKS

constexpr std::uint32_t MatchTuning::Timing::BACKGROUND_STOPPAGE_TICKS = 3
staticconstexpr

MatchFidelity::BACKGROUND merges up to this many fixed steps into one while the ball is dead before a throw-in, goal kick, corner, free kick or penalty (players only walk to their restart spots). Live play keeps the 10 Hz step: a coarser one measurably changes shots and goals.
