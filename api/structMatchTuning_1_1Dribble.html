Detailed Description

Dribbling: a carrier on the move pushes the ball 0.6-3 m ahead and catches it up at the next touch, so the ball is exposed between touches and a heavy touch can lose it. Defenders jockey goal-side and time their challenge for the moment the ball is away from the attacker's foot.
