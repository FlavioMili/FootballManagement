Detailed Description

Daylight preset: an afternoon sun high over the main stand's left shoulder, a brighter sward and a blue sky. Chosen from the kick-off time (DAY_FROM_MINUTES to DAY_UNTIL_MINUTES) or by the view's day toggle.
