Detailed Description

Academy registration of one player.

Academy players remain club players (wages, contracts, training and persistence work as for everyone else) but are listed apart from the senior squad (Team::getAcademyIDs, GameData::getAcademyForTeam); the record tells the U18 squad, trialists and graduates apart and carries youth match statistics.
