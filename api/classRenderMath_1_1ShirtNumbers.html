Detailed Description

Hands out shirt numbers for one team in a match. Starters get the classic number of their role when it is free (keeper 1, full backs 2 and 3, centre backs 4 and 5, holding midfielder 6, wide right 7, central 8, striker 9, playmaker 10, wide left 11), else the lowest free number up to 11; substitutes take 12 upwards. A number is never given out twice.
