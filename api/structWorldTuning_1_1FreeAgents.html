Detailed Description

The world has two divisions per country; players nobody signs drop below them (lower leagues, abroad) or stop playing. From September to May an unsigned free agent leaves the world with a monthly chance by age, scaled by his level against the weakest quarter of second-tier players. [P: most players released in the summer and still unsigned after the window sign at a lower level within the season]
