◆ aggravationMultiplier()

float InjuryModel::aggravationMultiplier ( InjurySeverity  severity)

How much more likely a player who plays carrying an injury is to break down than a fit player: x2 with a minor knock, x3 with a moderate injury and x4 with a major one (tissue that has not healed fails under match load).

Injury InjuryModel::draw ( WorldRng &  rng,
InjuryContext  context,
InjuryType  previous,
bool  recent_previous 
)

Draws a diagnosis and layoff. Diagnosis frequencies follow Ekstrand et al. (2020) with hamstrings up-weighted to ~24% (Ekstrand 2023); layoffs are lognormal with the published median and P10-P90 range. ACL injuries are twenty times rarer in training. A recent injury of the same type is more likely to recur and lasts 33% longer.
