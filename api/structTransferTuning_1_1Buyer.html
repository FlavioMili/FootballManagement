Detailed Description

An AI club negotiating for one of the managed club's players. Real deals are structured: add-ons are typically 10-30% of the headline fee, sell-on clauses 10-20%, and big fees are spread over 2-4 years. The buyer reads a structure by its present cost: deferred money is cheaper for it than cash (it discounts faster than a seller, which is why instalments help both sides), add-ons cost their odds of being paid and a sell-on costs the expected share of a future sale.
