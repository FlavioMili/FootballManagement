Detailed Description

An AI club negotiating to borrow one of the managed club's players. It reads a loan by its cost: the wage share it pays over the loan, the loan fee, a recall clause (it may lose him mid-season) and the odds of paying the fee for unplayed games. Its wage room caps the share it can take on. Concessions, patience, insults and deadline pressure follow the buyer (Buyer above). [P]
