Detailed Description

Selection rules shared by live and simulated matches: who may take part, how an ineligible selection is replaced, and how the players' persistent physical state enters and leaves the match engine.

)

Puts the regulars of Lineup::getStandIns() who may play again back in place of their stand-ins: from the bench the two swap, from outside the matchday squad the regular takes the stand-in's place. While a regular is still out, an eligible player who fills the slot clearly better (slotScore) replaces his stand-in. Entries whose regular already starts, whose stand-in no longer does or whose regular is not in squad are dropped. Returns the number of regulars recalled.
