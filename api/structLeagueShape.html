Detailed Description

How unequal the clubs of a league are in first-team quality.

Top divisions are far more stratified than second tiers: points per game spread 0.43-0.54 (SD over the table) against 0.28-0.36, a single hegemon in Germany and France, three giants in Spain and Portugal, a big six in England. [S: realism-research-2 2.1-2.2, FD-computed 2015-26]
