Monthly review of league position (vs target) and finances. Dismissal needs several consecutive critical reviews after enough matches, so only sustained, extreme failure ends the job. Out of the league season (in_season false) only the finances are reviewed and nobody is dismissed: the season is judged once, when it ends.
