◆ MatchFidelity

enum class MatchFidelity : std::uint8_t
strong

Simulation detail of a headless match (see MatchEngine::simulateToEnd).

Enumerator
FULL 

The live engine's 10 Hz fixed step: watched matches and quick results.

BACKGROUND 

Unwatched background fixtures: live play is identical to FULL, but the walk to each restart spot is simulated in coarser steps (MatchTuning::Timing::BACKGROUND_STOPPAGE_TICKS). Deterministic per seed and statistically equivalent to FULL; a given seed plays a different match than in FULL.
