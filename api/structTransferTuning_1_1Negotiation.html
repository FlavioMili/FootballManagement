◆ RISE_MAX_AGE

constexpr int TransferTuning::Negotiation::RISE_MAX_AGE = 29
staticconstexpr

Structure the agent asks for: a yearly rise for players up to RISE_MAX_AGE on multi-year deals (by ambition), appearance money as a share of the weekly wage (more for players unsure of a place) and a signing-on fee of some weeks of wage on a transfer. The structure is worth his flat ask: the base wage is lowered to match. [P]
