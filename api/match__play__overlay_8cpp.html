Play-mode overlay over a rendered match view: a ring under the active footballer with his name and condition above him, the power bar while a button is held, a lighter ring on the next switch candidate, an arrow at the view's edge when the active footballer is out of the picture, and a radar of the whole pitch at the bottom of the view. Draws through the renderer's own projection, so it fits the 2D and the 3D view alike, and allocates nothing.
