Detailed Description

Friendlies, tour and training camp of the managed club.

Every club plays the generated pre-season friendlies, one a week (spread from Tuesday to Sunday). Choosing an opponent swaps fixtures within that week (the managed club's old opponent meets the new opponent's old one on the day the new opponent was to play), so nobody plays twice a week or loses a match. Away friendlies against clubs from another country can be booked as tour dates.
