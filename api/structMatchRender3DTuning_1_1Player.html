Detailed Description

Low-poly footballer proportions in metres for the 1.80 m reference (top of the head at 1.80 m, shoulders about 0.5 m across the arms); a player is scaled by his real height. Animation is driven by ground speed: stride length grows with speed, the leg phase advances with the distance actually covered on screen, and slow players idle. Swing amplitudes come from the Gait shapes.
