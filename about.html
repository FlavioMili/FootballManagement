About the project

A club of your own.
A world to grow into.

A desktop football management game built around the decisions that make a club—and the matches that bring them to life.

The whole career

Manage a squad, set its tactics, scout and negotiate, develop young players and keep the club on solid financial ground. Domestic leagues, cups, continental competition and international football give your career room to grow.

Watch it. Play it. Understand it.

A headless match engine models movement, ball flight, fatigue and rules. The 2D tactical view and 3D broadcast view show the same simulation. Play mode lets you control an outfield player while the rest of the team follows your tactics.

Read the engine guide ↗

Open from the source up

The game's code is C++23 with SDL3, Dear ImGui and SQLite, released under GNU GPL v3. It supports Linux and macOS, with experimental Windows builds. The default world has fictional footballers and club identities, with optional contributor-name cameos.

The match settings and most tuning are currently C++ extension points; the world, players and translations use editable JSON. The docs explain what can be changed today and what is still planned.
